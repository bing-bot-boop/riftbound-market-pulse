Demand score

55% official RQ top-deck share, 30% KLX latest year published-list frequency, and 15% typical/average copies. Missing components contribute 0. The score is a discovery aid, not a claim about TCGplayer sales volume.

Prices

RiftHunt's free API supplies the full catalogue and a daily TCGplayer price export with separate normal/foil finishes and exact TCGplayer product links. This page stores local snapshots in your browser so it can show changes after you use it over time.

Tournament data

Riftbound Zone usage, win rates, winners and KLXCards card histories are checked automatically about once an hour. Refresh loads the latest collected tournament data. Source websites can publish late or change their layouts; failed checks keep the last successful data and its timestamp. Coverage includes published most-played cards, not every deck ever played. Selected best finishes and top archetypes come directly from KLXCards.

Important interpretation

A card marked “not published” is not assumed to have 0% play. Riftbound Zone publishes exact percentages for its most-played lists and selected categories; anything outside that list is left unknown. Likewise, exact marketplace-wide TCGplayer units sold are not exposed by the free data sources used here, so this tracker sends you to the exact TCGplayer product page to verify the sales/history information TCGplayer shows.
